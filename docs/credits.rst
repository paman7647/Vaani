Acknowledgements
----------------

Vaani builds on the shoulders of open-source projects and communities.
Notable technologies that enable the assistant:

- Sphinx and PyData Sphinx Theme (documentation)
- Vosk, SpeechRecognition, and PyAudio (speech recognition pipeline)
- pyttsx3 and other TTS engines (speech synthesis)
- RapidFuzz and spaCy/TextBlob (NLP utilities)
- yt-dlp and VLC/Pygame (media handling)
- DuckDuckGo and Wikipedia integrations (web search)
